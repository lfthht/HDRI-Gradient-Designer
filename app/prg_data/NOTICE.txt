Prague Sky Model data (subset)

Source: Prague Sky Model dataset, ground level (PragueSkyModelDatasetGround.dat, 107.6 MB)
        Copyright 2022 Charles University
        https://zenodo.org/records/19140728 — licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE.txt)
Model:  A. Wilkie, P. Vevoda, T. Bashford-Rogers, L. Hošek, T. Iser, M. Kolářová, T. Rittig, J. Křivánek:
        "A Fitted Radiance and Attenuation Model for Realistic Atmospheres", ACM TOG 40(4), 2021;
        P. Vevoda, T. Bashford-Rogers, M. Kolářová, A. Wilkie: "A Wide Spectral Range Sky Radiance Model",
        Pacific Graphics 2022.

Modifications: these files contain a subset of the dataset (the sky radiance channels used for luminance, the
sun transmittance at ground level and the solar spectrum), repacked and xz-compressed by tools/prg/build_bundle.py.
Values are unchanged. Polarisation data is not included.
